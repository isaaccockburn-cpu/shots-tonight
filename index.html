Method: team shots are projected from each side’s shot rate, the opponent’s shot suppression, and expected power-play minutes. Each player gets his share of those shots, and a probability model that allows for streaky games turns the expected number into probabilities. Trend compares a player’s last 5 games with his season average. Fair odds are the same chance written as American odds with no sportsbook margin, so a sportsbook’s price for the same outcome usually pays a bit less. Tier hit rate is how often players the model rated in the same percentage range actually reached the line in past games, and Tier odds writes that rate as odds. It shows how far to trust the model’s number in that range, so where the two disagree, the tier figure is the better guide. Hover over a tier figure for the details.
