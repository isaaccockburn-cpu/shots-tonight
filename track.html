How the model’s percentages have compared with what players actually did.

How to read this. Players are grouped by the chance the model gave them. If the model is well calibrated, about 60% of the players it rated 60% should reach the line.

On the chart, a dot on the dashed diagonal means the model matched reality. Above the line, the model rated those players too low. Below it, too high. Bigger dots are bigger groups, and faint dots are groups too small to trust.
